## Overview

Lookup is a DNS resolver and recursive lookup engine — walking the delegation chain from the root
servers down rather than deferring to an upstream resolver.

## Key Features

- **Recursive resolution** from the root, not a forwarding stub
- **Built for reliability** under the failure modes real DNS presents — timeouts, truncation, partial answers
